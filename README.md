# 🔆 LDR Based LED Control

## Mini Project Report

A simple automatic LED control circuit developed as a Course Mini Project in the **Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering**, Srinivasa Ramanujan Institute of Technology (Autonomous), Ananthapuramu.

## 📌 Project Title

**LDR BASED LED CONTROL**

## 📝 Abstract

This mini project demonstrates an Automatic LED Control System using an **LDR (Light Dependent Resistor)** and a **transistor**. The practical circuit responds to surrounding light conditions:

- ☀️ When the surrounding light is more, the **LED is OFF**.
- 🌙 When the surrounding light is less or dark, the **LED is ON**.

The project is simple and cost-effective and demonstrates the practical working of an LDR, transistor switching, and basic electronic control concepts.

## 🎯 Objective

To develop a simple automatic LED control circuit that changes its LED state according to the surrounding light conditions.

## ⚙️ Working Principle

The LDR senses the surrounding light level. Its electrical resistance changes with the amount of light falling on it. The LDR is used with the BC547 transistor and resistor to form a switching arrangement for the LED.

### Observed Operation

| Light Condition | LED State |
|---|---|
| More surrounding light | OFF |
| Less surrounding light / Dark | ON |

## 🔧 Methodology

1. Collect the required components.
2. Place the components on a breadboard.
3. Connect the LDR, resistor, BC547 transistor and LED as shown in the circuit.
4. Connect the 9 V battery supply.
5. Expose the LDR to different levels of surrounding light.
6. Observe the change in LED state.
