# Circuit Information

## Main Components

- 9 V battery
- LDR
- 100 kΩ resistor
- BC547 transistor
- LED

## Operating Condition

**More light → LED OFF**

**Less light / dark → LED ON**

The original report contains a general circuit diagram and practical breadboard photographs. Refer to the original report for the exact visual circuit arrangement.

## Practical Test

1. Assemble the circuit on a breadboard.
2. Connect the 9 V battery.
3. Expose the LDR to more light and observe the LED.
4. Reduce the light falling on the LDR.
5. Observe the LED turning ON under low-light conditions.
